## Section: FAQ

**Heading:** Hosting FAQ

1. **What are the hosting fees?**
   Hosting is charged per kWh consumed, varying by contract term and total capacity. Long-term contracts (12+months) get preferential rates. Contact us for a custom quote.

2. **Who owns the equipment?**
   You do. Equipment is 100% yours. You can request shipment at any time with 30 days notice.

3. **How is my equipment monitored?**
   24/7 real-time monitoring with automated alerts. Our technicians handle firmware updates, repairs, and optimization. You get full dashboard access to hashrate, temperature, and performance metrics.

4. **What happens if a miner goes down?**
   Our on-site team diagnoses and repairs hardware issues, typically within 24 hours. You're notified instantly via the SuperApp.

5. **Can I visit the facility?**
   Yes. Contact us to schedule a tour of any active facility.
